What these numbers mean

Page views count page loads. Sessions are estimated per browser tab after 30 minutes of inactivity; they are not unique people. Enquiry clicks count openings of your Google Form, not submitted forms or new clients. Portal clicks count interest or entry attempts, not successful logins. Repeat clicks can occur.

No IP addresses, names, email addresses, form answers or cross-site identifiers are stored in these analytics. Known bots and browsers requesting privacy protection are excluded where detectable. Blockers and JavaScript settings can cause undercounting. Your editor previews are excluded.
